Business Overview
Our team decided to opt for an e-commerce clothing website that will be available to locals of Mauritius only. The website will help to choose thousands of fashion products from established brands as well as local businesses. Named Vogue & Chic, our business is to provide home delivery as well as pick up locations throughout the island with delivery time of at most 5 to 7 days. Vogue & Chic customers and even guest users with fashion and clothing tips to better suit them.
 The website offers clothing options for men, women and kids of all ages including unisex wear. Consumers being regular customers and premium customers will have options to shop various categories like formal, casual, sportswear and activewear upon their search.
The chosen database is capable of recording all data of logged customers purchase to data pertaining to the supply-chain aspect of the business.
The database also registers supplier information, product catalogue, warehousing and inventory status of each product and personal details of customers and admins. Customers are also offered to upgrade their account to premium membership, where they can have access to more discounts, early access to sales and exclusive offers.
